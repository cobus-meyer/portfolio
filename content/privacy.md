# Privacy note

This is my personal site. I use analytics to see how many people visit and what they look at. The site has no accounts, ads or forms.

## The hedgehog

The analytics tool is [PostHog](https://posthog.com). The data goes to its EU servers.

## What it collects

- The pages you view and the things you click.
- Where you came from, your browser and your device type.
- Your IP address, which PostHog receives with each event.

Session recording and surveys are turned off for this site.

## Your choice

- **No choice yet, or Close.** Visits and clicks are counted. PostHog stores nothing in your browser, so each page you open looks like a new visitor.
- **Hire it.** Visits and clicks are counted. PostHog stores cookies and local data, so it can recognise a return visit.
- **Not a good fit.** Nothing else is sent to PostHog, and its stored data is removed from your browser.

Your browser remembers your choice in local storage, so the banner stays away. The map keeps its own display settings there too. None of that is sent anywhere.

[Change your cookie choice](#cookie-choice)

## Other services

Cloudflare hosts this site. Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts. Both receive your IP address when your browser requests a page or font.
